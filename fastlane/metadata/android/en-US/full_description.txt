<p><b>XtMapper</b> is an free and open source keymapper application in development for Bliss OS. It can be used to play certain Android games that require a touchscreen, with keyboard and mouse.</p><p>Currently working:</p><ul><li>Multi-touch emulation</li><li>Emulate a touch pointer with mouse - Useful for games that only accept touch events and not mouse clicks.</li><li>Emulate a D-pad with W,A,S,D or arrow keys</li><li>Keyboard events to touch - And editing config with GUI</li></ul>
