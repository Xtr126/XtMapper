<p><b>XtMapper</b> is an free and open source key-mapper application for Android. It maps keyboard and mouse inputs to touch.</p><p>Key features:</p><ul><li>Emulate a touch pointer with mouse - Useful for games that only accept touch events and not mouse clicks.</li><li>Emulate a D-pad with W,A,S,D or arrow keys</li><li>Visual editor for key-mappings</li></li><li>Per-app profiles support</li></li><li>And more..</li></ul>
